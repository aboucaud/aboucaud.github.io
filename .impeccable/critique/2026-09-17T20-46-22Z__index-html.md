## Design Specificity Verdict

Authored for this product — but the specificity decays as you scroll.

The top third could not be lifted onto another product. The hero sidebar is information design: six keyed blocks ordered Institution / Research teams / Experiments / Specialties / Links / Education is a physicist's business card, answering the questions a Rubin collaborator arrives holding in under three seconds with zero prose. The print stylesheet is authored for a real situation almost no portfolio imagines.

What is not product-specific, most interchangeable first: the contact section (centred headline, 32px rule, four equal cells — and the heaviest interactive object on the page); the carousel chrome (thirteen dots and two translucent arrows, generic marketing vocabulary applied to the one asset PRODUCT.md calls "the argument"); the nav; and "Selected work" as a content-free section label.

The page gets less specific exactly as the visitor gets closer to the action it wants them to take.

**Deterministic scan:** 7 findings from the static scanner. Four `cramped-padding` findings are false positives with two distinct causes — the scanner is not media-aware and merged `@media print { padding: 0 }` with screen borders, producing states the browser never renders; and it ignores child padding (`.carousel`'s caption sits 20px from the edge inside `.carousel-slide`). The decisive check: the in-page detector, reading real computed styles from the live DOM, reports **zero** `cramped-padding`. `marketing-buzzword` is a false positive — "next-generation sky surveys" is standard terminology for the Stage-IV facility class, and the scanner truncated "deblending" to "lending" building its context window. `overused-font` is true (Space Grotesk measured at 92% of text). `all-caps-body` is technically true but mis-scoped: the 35 characters are the hero role line, which the rule's own text exempts as a label.

**In-page detector** added one finding the static scan missed: `buried-raster`, the 12 non-active slides at `opacity: 0` — inherent to a crossfade carousel.

**Visual overlays:** script injection succeeded and the detector ran in the page, but Chrome ran headless, so no overlay was displayed to a human. Console findings only.

## Overall Impression

This is a genuinely good page with one bad component and one misallocation of weight. The hero answers the visitor's real question above the fold; the photo band earns being the loud moment; the record reads as competent without a word of self-praise. Against that: the accent colour is ambiguous about what is clickable, the carousel's control layer defaces its own captions on mobile, and the page's heaviest interactive object serves the outcome PRODUCT.md explicitly rules out as success.

The single biggest opportunity is inverting the weight: the links that are the stated goal are the smallest type on the page, and the contact panel that is explicitly not the goal owns the last impression.

## What's Working

**The hero sidebar is information design, not a template rail.** Ten links and two degrees fit in 660px without reading as a list, because the 2.5px sage rule, 11px tracked key and 0.5px closing hairline give every block an unambiguous start and end.

**The print stylesheet.** ~100 lines nobody will see that make the page correct where this audience actually uses it. Every href expands after its link text; contact rows are suppressed so addresses don't print twice; label tracking is reset so URLs don't print in small caps.

**The carousel's pause and loading logic** (not its controls). Five stop conditions collapsed into one predicate, so no combination of hovering, scrolling away and back leaves a timer running. The `data-bg` deferral is the same discipline applied to bytes.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] The accent colour tells the truth in one column and lies in the other
`.hero-bio strong` is `var(--accent)` — the exact colour of every link. "CNRS Nucléaire & Particules" renders green in the bio and is not a link; 40px right, the identical string in the sidebar is. Verified: 0 anchors inside `.hero-bio`, 2 `<strong>`, and `.hero-bio a` is dead CSS. `.talk-title:hover` turns two entirely link-free rows accent-green. This violates the site's own One Accent Rule, which DESIGN.md contradicts three lines above by listing "emphasized names inside the bio".
**Fix:** make them links or strip the colour; delete `.talk-title:hover` and the dead `.hero-bio a`; resolve the DESIGN.md contradiction in favour of the rule.
**Command:** `/impeccable clarify`

### [P1] The carousel's dots deface the caption on mobile
Measured at 390px: the dot rail overlaps the caption by 208 × 17px — the marks run through "Observatory at twilight · Cerro Pachón". DESIGN.md states captions are content, not decoration, and PRODUCT.md fixes the credits verbatim because they name real people. This is a regression introduced when the dots were enlarged from 5px to 16×34 tap targets: the taller hit area pushed the mark into the caption line. Compounding it: 16px pitch fails WCAG 2.5.8 spacing on the one platform where arrows are hidden, and 15 consecutive tab stops sit between hero and projects with no arrow-key support.
**Fix:** move the rail to its own strip below the photo, or replace it with a `04 / 13` counter. Roving tabindex so the rail is one tab stop; bind ArrowLeft/Right; keep arrows visible on touch.
**Command:** `/impeccable harden`

### [P1] The heaviest interactive object serves the stated non-goal
The contact section owns a full-width bordered panel, four hover-lit cells, its own centred composition and the final impression. PRODUCT.md: "contact is not the success metric." The project links that are the metric are 11px uppercase with a 0.5px underline — the smallest type on the page.
**Fix:** invert the weights. Demote contact to a single row above the footer; give project links a real affordance at `--t-supporting`. Consider ending the page on routing rather than on contact.
**Command:** `/impeccable layout`

### [P2] The one uncopyable claim is set in the same grey as everything else
"In 2024–2025 I spent a year on-site in Chile commissioning the filter exchange system of the LSST camera (see photos below)" sits as the last bio line at body size in muted ink, with no terminal period, above ~270px of empty white. The photographs that prove it are 400px further down in a band with no visible name. The design is delegating to copy what composition should do.
**Fix:** fill the void with the argument — a photograph in the hero's empty half, or set the Chile sentence as a weighted lede, or give the band a visible name.
**Command:** `/impeccable shape`

### [P2] Link labels don't survive being read out of context
Four project links are labelled "Website". A screen-reader user hears "Website, Website, Website, Website". The photo band has an `aria-label` but no visible heading — the only region without a name a sighted visitor can read.
**Fix:** name the destination, not the type. Give the band a visible section name, which also costs the bio its "(see photos below)" crutch.
**Command:** `/impeccable audit`

## Minor Observations

- `.hero-bio` is one `<p>` using `<br><br>` for a paragraph break; screen readers and the printout get one run-on paragraph. Use two `<p>`.
- Missing terminal period on "(see photos below)" — the only sentence without one.
- `<em>` renders as browser-synthesized oblique; only `font-style: normal` ships for both faces.
- `goTo()` discards input for the full 1000ms with no acknowledgement. Move the dot's active class immediately even if the fade is queued.
- `img/profile.png` is 910KB, in the repo, referenced nowhere (excluded from deploy).
- At desktop the first slide is fetched twice: `index.html` inlines the small copy as the no-JS fallback, then the loader swaps in the full-size file. ~10KB wasted per desktop visit.
- The `body::before` backdrop reads well at 1728 and poorly at 1280 (an anonymous brown blur in a 160px gutter). It is the only non-factual element on a page whose north star is that everything is a fact. DESIGN.md already marks it provisional — worth a decision rather than drift.
- DESIGN.md contradicts itself: the One Accent Rule says links/tags/actions only, while the Observatory Sage entry lists "emphasized names inside the bio".
- No active state in the nav, and no nav entry for the photographs — the one section PRODUCT.md calls the argument is the one you cannot navigate to.
- `--subtle` measures 4.74:1 on paper: AA with ~5% headroom. Any future warming of the paper breaks it silently.
- Caption punctuation drifts between `·` and ` - `.
