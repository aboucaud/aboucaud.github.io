Alexandre Boucaud

Astronomer · Computational Engineer

I build open-source software and pipelines for large-scale astronomical experiments, at the intersection of signal processing, machine learning, and observational cosmology.
I work for the CNRS Nucléaire & Particules and am currently based at AstroParticule & Cosmologie (APC) laboratory in Paris, where I lead the IT department Computing and data engineering group.

In 2024–2025 I spent a year on-site in Chile commissioning the filter exchange system of the LSST camera (see photos below)

Projects & Experiments

Selected work

LSST / Rubin Observatory

Camera control software and commissioning for the Legacy Survey of Space and Time at Vera C. Rubin Observatory. On-site mission in Chile, Nov 2024 – Dec 2025.

Website Observatory tour (in French)

Euclid

Deep learning methods for galaxy image analysis and weak lensing pipelines for ESA's Euclid dark energy mission. Science ground segment integration at IAS and APC.

Website

AstroDeep

ANR-funded research programme applying deep learning to galaxy morphology, weak lensing, and image deblending for next-generation sky surveys (LSST, Euclid).

Website

Lightcone Research

Open-source infrastructure for transparent and verifiable computational science assisted with agentic AI, all based on the ASTRA specification.

Website GitHub
